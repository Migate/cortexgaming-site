Neke The Snake

The classic snake game on a retro LCD screen.

What you get

  • Swipe to steer, or play with the number keys.
  • Nine speed levels, and walls you can switch on or off.
  • Five maps: Open, Box, Tunnels, Pillars and Windmill.
  • Big apples are worth double the level number.
  • A leaderboard for each map through Google Play Games.
  • Beeps and vibration, both easy to turn off.
