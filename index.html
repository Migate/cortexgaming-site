We build arcade games the classic way: a clear goal, a challenge that keeps rising, and a score worth beating. Then we keep refining them until they feel right under your thumb.

  • Simple to learn

    A few seconds is all it takes to understand the rules, and the controls stay out of your way.

  • A score to beat

    Every run ends with a number, and a good reason to go again.

  • Made small

    Light and quick to open, so a game fits into any spare minute.

How we make a game

  1. Prototype

    We start with a rough, playable version and find out whether the idea is fun.

  2. Playtest

    Real players try it. Whatever confuses them gets fixed or cut.

  3. Polish

    Timing, sound and feel are tuned until every tap lands right.

  4. Release

    The game goes live on Google Play, and we keep improving it with updates.
